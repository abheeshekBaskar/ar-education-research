## Objective

To investigate how Augmented Reality (AR) technology influences immersion, engagement, and learning outcomes among university students in science education — and to develop a validated model explaining the factors that drive student flow experiences in AR environments.

---

## Research Topic

**Augmented Reality (AR) in Education: Applications and Challenges**

AR integrates virtual elements into the real world, creating interactive and immersive learning environments. This paper reviews AR's historical development, its educational applications across disciplines, and the psychological factors — usability, emotional investment, focus of attention, presence, and flow — that determine how effectively students engage with AR tools.

---

## Methodology

| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Research Design | Correlational method |
| Analysis Tool | Structural Equation Model (SEM) |
| Sample Size | 180 university science education students (32M / 148F) |
| Data Collection | ARI Questionnaire (Georgiou & Kyza, 2017) — 21 items, 7-point Likert scale |
| Dimensions | Total Immersion, Engrossment, Engagement |
| Sub-factors | Flow, Presence, Emotional Investment, Focus of Attention, Usability, Interest |
| Reliability | Cronbach's α = 0.913 |

---

## Key Findings

- **All 6 research hypotheses were supported** by SEM analysis
- The model explains **71% of variance** in university students' flow experiences
- **Focus of attention** and **presence** directly influence flow
- **Emotional investment** and presence influence focus of attention
- **Usability** and emotional investment influence student interest
- **99% of students** used smartphones — underscoring the need for mobile-first AR development
- **76%** had never heard of AR before the study — highlighting the novelty effect
- AR-based learning environments produced **higher test scores** than web-based alternatives (Mexican school study, Ibáñez et al., 2019)

## Research Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Statement | Result |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| H1 | Usability positively affects students' interest | ✅ Supported |
| H2 | Emotional investment positively affects students' interest | ✅ Supported |
| H3 | Focus of attention & presence influence flow experiences | ✅ Supported |
| H4 | Emotional investment & presence influence focus of attention | ✅ Supported |
| H5 | Usability & emotional investment influence student interest | ✅ Supported |
| H6 | Positive attitude toward digital learning → behavioral intention to use AR/VR | ✅ Supported |
